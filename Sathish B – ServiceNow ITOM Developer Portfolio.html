About

I build event-driven operations on ServiceNow. I have integrated multiple monitoring sources through Push and Pull connectors, REST APIs and Scripted REST APIs, then shaped the data with Event Rules, Alert Management Rules and field mapping.

Advanced correlation (tag-based, CMDB-based, rule-based and cross-source) groups related alerts so teams see fewer, more meaningful ones. I have also implemented AIOps LEAP Playbooks to shorten incident resolution paths and reduce manual work.

Before ServiceNow development, I worked on CMDB data governance and data analytics, which is why I care about clean, trustworthy data.
